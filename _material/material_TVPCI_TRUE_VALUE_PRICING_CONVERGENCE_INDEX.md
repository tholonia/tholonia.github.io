The TVPCI is a dimensionless composite index (0–100) that measures how closely the structural condition of a supply chain allows an observed market price to converge toward its True Value. It quantifies friction, imbalance, and incoherence across supply chain phases using the C-G-N Tholonic model — the primary structural output of the TrueValue Analytics platform. When TVPCI = 100 the chain operates at maximum structural integrity; as it falls, divergence between market price and True Value is structurally explained.
